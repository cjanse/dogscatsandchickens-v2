# Dogs, Cats & Chickens — Official Rules

*A card game of pets, pranks, and pecking order for 2 players.*

---

## Object of the Game

Build a field of creatures and use them to knock out every creature your opponent has. Protect your own creatures with **Upgrades**, and outwit your opponent with **Actions**.

**You win when your opponent has no creatures left — none on their field and none in their hand.**

## The Play Area

Each player has:

- **Hand** — cards you hold. Keep them hidden from your opponent.
- **Field** — the creatures you have in play, with any upgrades tucked underneath them.

Shared between both players:

- **Deck** — face-down draw pile.
- **Discard pile** — face-up pile of used and defeated cards. Either player may look through it at any time.

---

## Setup

1. Shuffle the deck.
2. Each player takes **3 Creature cards at random** from the deck.
3. Shuffle again, and each player draws **2 more cards** from the top of the deck.
4. Each player now has a starting hand of **5 cards**.
5. Decide who goes first — pick a player, or choose at random.

---

## Face-Down and Face-Up Cards

Every card you put on your field — creatures and upgrades — is played **face down**. Your opponent knows *that* you have a creature, but not *which* creature, and not what its upgrades are.

Cards turn **face up** when:

- a creature **attacks or is attacked** (both creatures are revealed),
- a **Power of Tea** reveals it,
- a matched pair's **ability is activated** (see Matching), or
- a matched **Chicken** is activated, which flips every card on the opponent's field (see Matching Abilities).

Once face up, a card stays face up until it leaves the field. Cards going to the discard pile, or back to a hand, are no longer considered revealed.

---

## Turn Order

### Your First Turn

On your very first turn, **place one creature face down on your field**, then end your turn. You don't draw and you take no other moves.

### Every Turn After That

Each turn has three steps:

#### 1. Draw

Draw **one card** from the top of the deck. You must draw before doing anything else. If the deck is empty, skip this step.

#### 2. Choose: Attack Turn *or* Reinforce Turn

You have **2 moves** per turn. Pick one kind of turn:

**⚔️ Attack Turn** — uses your whole turn (both moves).
Choose one of your creatures and one of your opponent's creatures. Your creature attacks it (see *Battles*). You may not attack after you have already used a move this turn.

**🛡️ Reinforce Turn** — make up to **2 moves**, in any combination:

- Place a creature on your field (face down).
- Match a creature (see *Matching*).
- Attach an upgrade to one of your creatures.
- Play an action card.

You may end your turn early without using all your moves.

#### 3. Clean Up

If you have **more than 5 cards** in hand, discard down to 5. Then end your turn.

---

## Battles

When your creature attacks, flip both creatures face up and compare types:

| Attacker vs. Defender | Result |
|---|---|
| **Attacker's type beats defender's** (Cat→Chicken, Dog→Cat, Chicken→Dog) | Defender is defeated — *unless* an upgrade saves it (see *Upgrades*). |
| **Defender's type beats attacker's** | The **attacker** is defeated. The attacker's own upgrades do **not** help — the attacker and everything attached to it go to the discard pile. |
| **Same type** | Nothing happens. |

> **Remember the circle:** Dogs chase Cats, Cats chase Chickens, Chickens chase Dogs.

A defeated creature goes to the discard pile along with all of its attached upgrades.

---

## Matching

When you have a creature on your field, you can place its **identical twin** (same name, e.g. Lulu on Lulu) on top of it. This uses **one move**.

- The two cards become a **matched pair** and are treated as **one creature** from now on — they attack, defend, and are defeated together.
- Placing the match does **not** automatically reveal it. If the pair is still face down, you may **activate** its ability at any time on your turn, which flips the pair face up. (Activating does not cost a move.) If the bottom creature was already face up, the ability activates immediately.
- A matched pair whose ability is active is face up for all to see.

### Matching Abilities

| Type | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Cat** | *Multiple Attacks* | On an Attack Turn, this same cat (the matched pair) may attack again and again, choosing a target each time, for as long as it survives. No other creature may attack again. Stop whenever you like. |
| **Dog** | *Defeats Any Creature* | This dog wins every battle it starts, even against a Chicken. (Upgrades on the defender still apply.) |
| **Chicken** | *Opponent Must Have All Face-Up Cards* | When activated, **every** card on the opponent's field flips face up. This happens once, when the pair is activated. Cards the opponent plays later are still placed face down. |

> A matched Dog that attacks a Chicken protected by **Counter Attack** is itself defeated — see below.

---

## Upgrades

Attach upgrades face down under one of your creatures. Each attachment uses **one move**.

- Each creature (or matched pair) can carry **at most 2 upgrades**.
- Upgrades do nothing until their creature **would be defeated by an attack**. Only then does an upgrade trigger.
- **Only the most recently attached upgrade triggers first.** Think of the upgrades as a stack — the top one answers the attack.

| Upgrade | When your creature would be defeated… |
|---|---|
| **Defense** | Blocks the attack. Your creature survives. Discard the Defense. |
| **Counter Attack** | Blocks the attack. Your creature survives. Discard the Counter Attack. If your creature's type normally beats the attacker's (e.g. your Chicken was attacked by a matched Dog), the attacker is defeated as well. |
| **Fake Upgrade** | Does nothing to stop the attack — but instead of being discarded, it **returns to your hand**. Then the next upgrade underneath (if any) gets its chance to trigger; if there is none, your creature is defeated. Great for bluffing! |
| **Revive** | Your creature is defeated, but instead of going to the discard pile, it **returns to your hand** (both cards, if a matched pair). Revive saves only the creature: the Revive and any other upgrade on it are discarded — even a Fake Upgrade, which does **not** go back to your hand in this case. |
| **Self-Destruct** | Your creature is defeated — and it takes the **attacker** down with it. Both creatures and all of their upgrades go to the discard pile. |

---

## Actions

Playing an action card uses **one move**. After it resolves, discard it.

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Beach Spirits** | Take one **Upgrade** of your choice from the discard pile into your hand. |
| **Forest Spirits** | Take one **Creature** of your choice from the discard pile into your hand. |
| **River Spirits** | Take one **Action** of your choice from the discard pile into your hand. |
| **Bird Army** | Choose a card kind (Creature, Upgrade, or Action). Steal one card of that kind **at random** from your opponent's hand. |
| **Messy Dorm** | Look at your opponent's entire hand. |
| **Power of Tea** | Choose one face-down card on your opponent's field and flip it face up. (If it's part of a matched pair, both cards flip. Revealing a matched Chicken activates its ability!) |

An action can only be played when it can actually do something (e.g. Forest Spirits needs a creature in the discard pile; Power of Tea needs a face-down card on the opponent's field).

---

## Recovery Turn — Coming Back From the Brink

If you begin a turn with **no creatures on your field**, you are in trouble! You still have your **2 moves**, but you **cannot draw** until you have a creature on your field. Use your moves to get one there:

- Place a **creature** from your hand, or
- Play **Forest Spirits** to recover a creature from the discard pile, or
- Play **Bird Army** to steal a random creature from your opponent's hand.

(For example: Forest Spirits for your first move, then place the recovered creature with your second.)

As soon as a creature is on your field, you may draw your card, then use any move you have left as normal.

**If you end your turn with no creatures on your field, you lose.**

---

## Winning, Losing & Ties

At the end of each turn, check:

- **Win:** Your opponent has **no creatures on their field and no creatures in their hand**.
- **Lose:** You end your turn with **no creatures on your field**.
- **Tie:** The deck is empty, neither player can play any more cards, and every creature left on the field is the **same type** (so no one can ever defeat anyone). The game is a draw.

## Quick Reference

```
FIRST TURN:   Place 1 creature. End turn.

EACH TURN:    1. Draw 1 card
              2. ATTACK (whole turn)  — or —  REINFORCE (2 moves)
                                              • Place creature
                                              • Match creature
                                              • Attach upgrade
                                              • Play action
              3. Discard down to 5 cards. End turn.

BATTLE:       Dog > Cat > Chicken > Dog   (same type = no effect)
UPGRADES:     Max 2 per creature. Trigger only when defeated. Top one first.
MATCHING:     Same-name twin on top. Counts as one creature. Flip to activate.
WIN:          Opponent has no creatures on field or in hand.
```
